# Rental turnover cleaning

Use this sequence for the two rentals: one two-bedroom, one-bath unit and one one-bedroom, one-bath unit. Both have small kitchens and bathrooms and substantial glass to clean. Start long-running machines first, work from high surfaces to low surfaces, and finish with floors.

1. Inspect for damage and unusually dirty areas. Strip beds, collect towels and start laundry. Start the dishwasher where available.
2. Collect trash, leftover food, dishes and misplaced items in one pass. Carry a cleaning caddy and waste bag to reduce trips.
3. Apply bathroom cleaners that need time to work, following their labels. Complete nearby tasks during the waiting time.
4. Dust bedrooms and living areas from high to low. Make beds after nearby dusting, with clean hands and fresh linens.
5. Clean glass in a dedicated pass. Clean frames first, then panes, then lower edges. Use glass-safe cleaner, clean microfiber cloths and a squeegee for large panes; check from an angle for streaks.
6. Finish the kitchen, then the bathroom. Work through kitchen appliances and counters and finish with the sink. In the bathroom, finish the shower, mirror and sink before the toilet. Keep bathroom and kitchen cloths separate.
7. Restock towels, toilet paper, soap, coffee and bin liners; arrange pillows and other guest supplies.
8. Vacuum, then mop toward the exit. Finish with a guest-eye check for hair, crumbs, streaks, odors, missing supplies and bed presentation.

If the rentals are adjacent, start machines in both, then finish one unit at a time. With two cleaners, divide by zones: kitchens and bathrooms versus bedrooms, living areas and glass. Adapt to actual laundry capacity and checkout deadlines.

Where disinfecting is needed, clean first and keep the surface wet for the product label's contact time. See [CDC cleaning and disinfection guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/hygiene/about/when-and-how-to-clean-and-disinfect-a-facility.html). Working high to low avoids re-soiling cleaned areas; see [CDC cleaning preparation guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/project-firstline/media/pdfs/Micro-Learns-EVS-Cleaning-Disinfection-508.pdf).
