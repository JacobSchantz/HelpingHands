## Turnover sequence

Start long-running machines first, work from high surfaces to low surfaces,
and finish with floors.

1. Inspect for damage and unusually dirty areas. Photograph damage before touching it. Strip beds, collect towels and start laundry. Start the dishwasher where available.
2. Collect trash, leftover food, dishes and misplaced items in one pass. Carry a cleaning caddy and waste bag to reduce trips.
3. Apply bathroom cleaners that need time to work, following their labels. Complete nearby tasks during the waiting time.
4. Dust bedrooms and living areas from high to low. Make beds after nearby dusting, with clean hands and fresh linens.
5. Clean glass in a dedicated pass. Clean frames first, then panes, then lower edges. Use glass-safe cleaner, clean microfiber cloths and a squeegee for large panes; check from an angle for streaks.
6. Finish the kitchen, then the bathroom. Work through kitchen appliances and counters and finish with the sink. In the bathroom, finish the shower, mirror and sink before the toilet. Keep bathroom and kitchen cloths separate.
7. Restock towels, toilet paper, soap, coffee and bin liners; arrange pillows and other guest supplies.
8. Vacuum, then mop toward the exit.

If the rentals are adjacent, start machines in both, then finish one unit at a
time. With two cleaners, divide by zones: kitchens and bathrooms versus
bedrooms, living areas and glass. Adapt to actual laundry capacity and
checkout deadlines.

## Before you leave: verification

Walk the unit once as a guest would, door to bedroom, and check each item.

- Beds made tight with fresh linens, pillows arranged, no hair on pillowcases.
- No crumbs on counters, table, floor edges or inside the microwave.
- Glass and mirrors streak-free when viewed from an angle.
- Shower, sink and toilet clean, including the toilet base and behind it.
- No odors; trash and recycling empty with fresh liners.
- Towels, toilet paper, soap, coffee and bin liners stocked.
- Dishes clean and put away; dishwasher and fridge empty of guest food.
- Lights, thermostat and windows set the way the next guest should find them.
- Damage or missing items reported to Jake with a photo.

## Surfaces and cleaning methods

Glass: glass-safe cleaner, clean microfiber, squeegee on large panes, frames
before panes. Disinfecting: clean first, then keep the surface wet for the
product label's contact time. See the CDC facility cleaning and disinfection
guidance at cdc.gov/hygiene/about/when-and-how-to-clean-and-disinfect-a-facility.html.
Work high to low so cleaned areas are not re-soiled.
