# gen_05 original-shell experiment — stopped, incomplete

Jake redirected this work to inspecting actual Aero Hand pieces and scaling them before pursuing a wearable redesign. This generation is preserved as an unaccepted experiment.

**Do not print or use this package.** The most recent executed audit found straight-pose guide/adjacent-part intersections of 0.38 mm³ (proximal/middle) and 9.11 mm³ (middle/distal). The source then received unvalidated spring-retention and dorsal-lead edits. Exported meshes are stale relative to the current source. No physical test or actuation took place, and no spring-rate, strength or wearable-fit claim is made.

The original geometry, checker and provisional files are preserved for possible future study. The new Aero reference/scaling work is separate and subject to its upstream hardware license.
