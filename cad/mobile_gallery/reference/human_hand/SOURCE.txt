  A skinned hand on the 25 WebXR hand joints (wrist, 4 thumb, 5 per finger), metres, rest pose flat.
  As shipped: wrist joint to the farthest skin along the middle finger 189.7 mm; index-to-pinky knuckle centres 58.6 mm
  (about 79 mm across the knuckles), a slightly narrow hand for its length.

Size: ANSUR II (US Army 2012 anthropometric survey, public data, 4,082 men / 1,986 women), handlength = wrist crease
  (stylion) to the middle fingertip (dactylion):
    men   193.3 mm (sd 9.9)   hand breadth 88.3 mm
    women 181.1 mm (sd 10.1)  hand breadth 78.2 mm
    "average adult" = their unweighted mean, 187.2 mm
  The viewer scales the mesh uniformly (proportions kept) so wrist joint to fingertip is the chosen length. Breadth then
  comes out ~78 mm for the adult size, a few mm under the ANSUR means.
  Data: https://www.openlab.psu.edu/ansur2/ (mirror used: github.com/senihberkay/US-Army-ANSUR-II).

Articulation (human_hand.js): each finger bends at its three joints about the joint's own X axis (WebXR: -Z along the bone,
  +Y out of the back of the hand). Full curl, degrees: thumb 30/40/65 (base, knuckle, end), index 80/100/70, middle 85/100/70,
  ring 88/100/70, pinky 90/100/70 (knuckle, middle, end joint). No sideways spread or thumb opposition yet.
